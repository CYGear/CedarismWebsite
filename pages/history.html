It is often theorized that according to the ancient
texts, Antioch was located along the Tigris or
Euphrates river, considering the city of Gens Cedri
(Babylon) laid on these rivers. For all of the first
books, the setting stays in Antioch, rarely moving,
except to go to the North ruins.

The north ruins are theorized to be Kish, due to
the fact that Kish was a pre-babylonian civilization
that was 8 miles to the north of the future site of
Gens Cedri.
