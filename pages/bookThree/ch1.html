Malum Surgit (The Rise of Evil) Chapter 1

 In the 7th or 8th generation of The Tribe
of Antioch, there was an Arch Bishop
of the later generations, a direct descendant
of Lauren and Tiffany of the second generation
.1

He was a well made priest, the greatest arguably
of the line of Arch-Bishops from Lauren and Tiffany,
a great preacher of the church
.2

He personally helped with the construction
of the new temple of Cederism,
the now blueprint for modern temple constructions
.3

The Arch-Bishop was named Gianni,
highest of Arch-Bishops of the beginning
.4

 And it was his religious power that was necessary,
between them all to ward off the coming evil
towards Cederism
.5

 And it was in the ninth month of the year,
on a cold day, the sixth of the month,
when the evil showed its true face
unto the Cedarists
.6

The ground split open in one particular spot,
with her majesty of evilness rising
with red light around her
.7

And this was her appearance to the Cedarists,
watching The Lady of Evil rise
and show herself to them
.8

 She was in a long red dress,
with a crown of red thorns surrounding her head,
extending from her head and around it
.9

She gazed upon them with an evil stare,
and began to speak:
.10

 “Followers of The Cedar!
.11

You shall not reach your salvation in the Cedarism,
for The Lord Cedar, curse his name,
has killed all my brethren and forefathers!
.12

He hath sent me down to the forest of the dead,
where the evil doers lie,
and cursed me to watch over them
.13

But for why?
.14

For nothing!”
.15

 And the Cedarists were quite appalled
by the Queen of Evil’s words,
and she revealed her true name, Mariana,
Queen of Evil and Ruler of The Dead
.16

 It was the Queen of evil that inspired
the Prophetai to begin the writings
of what was truly evil in Cederism,
and so they wrote down what Cederus said
to them was unholy
.17
