Chapter 2

 And so the prophets wrote from The Lord Cedar
and the lorekeeper scribeth unto the paper,
these true evils
.1

 You shall not become hateful to another
for their difference to you, for their similarity
is even greater than you think
.2

As must be repeated, the greatest evil
is the harm of a living plant
.3

Doing so makes one a follower of Mariana,
and you shall eternally spend your days
in The Forest of The Dead, trapped within a tree
kicking and screaming, thirsty, hungry,
but unable to die
.4

 Non-believers who do no harm shall not join
the evil-doers in The Forest of The Dead,
for instead they shall go to a wonderful city
where they may walk with their families
who did not believe, and live peacefully
.5

For The Lord Cedar did not want to punish
the just only for not worshipping its existence,
so it created the third area, where neither
greatness nor darkness could reach them
.6

 And it was officially made that the 6th day
of the 9th month of the year shall be the day
of evil, and no worship shall proceed on that day
due to the remembrance of the arrival
of the Anti-Cedar
.7

 It still befuddled the Cederists why Mariana
had been cast away by Cedrus,
for he was benevolent and loving
.8

Soren and The Prophets took a quest
to figure out what had truly transpired
in order for such an occurrence to be true
.9
