Chapter 4

 And the group traveled back to the land of
Antioch, where Gianni and Samuel began the
construction of the great church of Antioch, the
basis for all of the future churches to come
.1


 The church was in a hexagon shape, to
symbolize the enlightenment of those sitting in
the outer hexagon, watching down on the Cedar
Tree in the inner hexagon, which the Prophets
and Bishops circled on steps to speak to the followers
.2


 And the main church hexagon was surrounded
by smaller hexagons which contained offices and
facilities needed for the church, always in
hexagonal shape
.3

And it became that the hexagon was the symbol
of the Cederists, for the hexagon was the
symbol of their enlightenment
.4
