Chapter 3

 The journey was directed by the visions
of the prophets, for they were never wrong
.1

Under the guidance of The Lord Cedar through
visions, the company of 5 set out into the
world to find the answers
.2


 The company included Soren, Orion, Gianni,
Dorinda (The Other Prophet of the time), and
Samuel, the highest of the ordered bishops
.3


 Now you may wonder how Orion and Soren had
survived 3 generations, having been born in
the fifth generation
.4

The explanation comes, that the world was pure,
and true plentiful evil had not yet risen to
hurt the peoples
.5

The wars and effort of the first and second
generation had shortened their lifespans, and
given them less time to worship Cederus
.6

But this was when little evil existed, and the
wonders of Cederism were plentiful through the
land of Antioch
.7


 First the party went to the north as the
Prophetai had foreseen happenings there
.8

It was Orion and Dorinda in the night who had
visions, and they were shown this:

 (Note, the following text comes from a
translated poem written by Orion in the 10th
century describing what he saw that night)
.9


 In the tomb north
 Lay the carved scrolls
 They speak of the events
 The evils of Mariana
 Her treachery
 Her great harms
 Find them and 
 The answers you seek
 Will be yours.
.10

 Upon the forest it lies
 Covered in dirt
 From the old settlement
 In the center,
 10 feet under
 Dig and you shall find it.
.11


 And with this vision, the group set off
towards the north, towards the old settlement
.12

The old settlement was the other settlement,
the non-cederists of the war over the river
.13


 Their settlement was now a stone ruin,
vines all among the area, for in their venture
south to Antioch and to the river they had all
perished in the war of their causing
.14


 Eventually the troupe reached the old
settlement, and found its ruins, covered in
foliage. In the center of the settlement there
was a Cedar Tree, withered and dead
.15


 The group dug around the tree, under it ten
feet down as Cedrus had commanded, and hit stone
.16

They dug all around until all of the stone was
unearthed, and they had found an opening
.17


 Inside the temple was an anti-hexagon, with
the center being a stone hexagon with the
Cedar’s roots extending down into it
.18

In the tomb were coffins, and in them were
bodies long dead, seemingly murdered by a sharp
object such as a knife; but sitting by the
coffins were skeletons, rotted and gone, skinny
and thin, covered in webs
.19


 It was then that the group noticed the
inscription along the wall of the tomb
.20


 “It was in the night she attacked, murdering
our fellow townspeople quite randomly
.21

She had taken a long knife and stabbed four of
the men and women in their sleep, and kicked
them from their beds onto the cold ground and
watched them scream in terror as their lives ended
.22

We all burst in after we had heard the screams,
and saw the terrible scene that had occurred,
and said to her, Mariana, “Why have you
committed this terrible evil?”
.23


 And she responded, “My evil hath consumed
me, and I hath heard of something
.24

There was a knock and this knock, it slowly
formed words, the words, they became to say that
I should kill them, and then only the knocking
would cease, and now it has! The knocking hath ceased!
.25

Did you not hear the terrible knocking,
continuous, awful, noisy, loud?”
.26


 And it was then we knew that she had fallen
to the forces of evil, for she must have harmed
a plant like an evil-doer
.27

This belief was re-affirmed when we found the
chopped tree outside our settlement the next
day at dawn, with her axe sitting next to it
.28


 We knew she was dangerous, for she charged
at us as well and began to attempt at our lives
too, but we threw her into the old temple under
the Cedar, and sealed her in there, deep underground
.29


 And yet still she escaped, for we found the
next day she stood sitting in the Cedar, axe in hand
.30

And then she began to chop the Cedar with her
axe, committing the horrid crime
.31

We knew she was quite treacherous, so we chose
to flee
.32

Half of us, including myself, fled into the
temple with food stores and sealed ourselves
off in the tomb with the bodies of the dead
.33

And we placed the bodies in the ceremonial
coffins, for we hope they will be found someday
and given proper burial
.34

As for the rest of the tribe, they fled
somewhere south with packs of food and drink
.35

I only hope they find a good place to settle,
and are safe from the terror of Mariana
.36

If the gods are willing, we may make it out of
this tomb alive
.37


 And now the Cederists realized what had
truly occurred, and were enlightened
.38

And they stepped out of the tomb, and up into
the ruins and saw that a beam of bright sunlight
shone upon the now tall and magnificent Cedar,
for they had been enlightened with the knowledge
of the beginning of the evil, and now they could
do good to stop it
.39
