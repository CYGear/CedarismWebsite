Chapter 4

 And the third generation, having all grown
up now, made their own children
.1

And they taught the children well, and of The
Lord Cedar, and worshipped it, And The Lord
Cedar was pleased with this
.2

And the third generation saw that that The Lord
Cedar was pleased and created a temple to worship
Cedrus, and called it The First Temple of The
Order of Cedar, and they recorded The Lord Cedar’s
word, and this was how the Holy Book of Cedar
came to be, written by the Third, Fourth, Fifth
generations of Antioch, writing The Beginning of
the Holy Book, The Lord Cedar’s word, and we,
The Tribe of Antioch, have transcribed the word
of The Lord Cedar into paper and pen, giving
you its word
.3
