Chapter 3

 One day, working in the fields, Sebastian
and Tyler were having a discussion
.1

Soon discussion turned to argument, and argument
turned to fight
.2

They began to throw their hands, and it became
very heated
.3

Through the fields the two jumped at each other,
injuring each other greatly
.4

And eventually it came that Tyler emerged
victorious from the brutal battle, and Sebastian
was dead
.5


 The Lord Cedar did not often like interfering
in human affairs, but The Lord Cedar decided
enough was enough
.6

Tyler was dead now, next to Sebastian on the
fields, and The Lord Cedar took his body and
introduced him to the time after
.7

And The Lord Cedar said: “You have committed an
unjustified evil I have not accounted for
.8

Your evil shall be punished in a cruel way,
such as your cruel actions.”
.9

And The Lord Cedar created the eternal punishment,
such that all Evil do-ers would experience after
their demise
.10


 And for the rest of eternity Tyler spent his
days inside of a tree, with great allergy to its
pollen, trapped
.11

And The Lord Cedar watched his suffering, and
thought if he could have been better perhaps such
a punishment would not be needed
.12

But The Lord Cedar had given its creations
freedom, and with freedom came evil
.13


 And soon, the second generation began to pass
on, and they joined The Lord Cedar in the garden
of peace, and had beautiful wonders there
.14

The garden was beautiful in every sort of way,
wondrous beyond compare
.15

And they all went there, except for Tyler and
Sebastian, who were equally guilty of evil
.16
