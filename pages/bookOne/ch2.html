Chapter 2

 And the first men and women under The Lord
Cedar were of the Tribe of Antioch
.1

And the Tribe of Antioch consisted of 10 members:
Joshua, Joseph, Benjamin, Roman, Philip, Jane,
Mary, Elizabeth, Mia, and Zoe
.2

And each of the members were fruitful and
multiplied, and slowly the race of humans began
to take shape, and Cedrus watched this with great
happiness, as his creations began to bear fruit
and become an independent species of their own
.3

And out of each of the pairings of The Tribe
of Antioch were the children of Joshua and Jane:
Ronan, Sarah and Arnold
.4

And Followed this were the children of Joseph
and Mary: Liam, Emma, and Sophia
.5

And Followed this were the children of Benjamin
and Elizabeth: Sebastian, and Jamie
.6

And Followed this were the children of Roman
and Mia: Tyler, Christina, Lauren, and Hellen
.7

And Followed was the child of Philip and Zoe:
Tiffany
.8

And this was the second generation of the
children of Antioch
.9


 And far in the east there was a second tribe,
a brutal tribe
.10

And this tribe set out to explore out into the
land, and found the river of Antioch
.11

And the tribe said, This river is good, we
shall claim it
.12

But little did they know, the river was already
claimed by the Tribe of Antioch
.13

And so a dispute arose over the ownership of
the lake, and The Lord Cedar watched its own
creation’s nature arise, and was disgusted over
their actions, and for this, it created a punishment
.14

And all of the first generation of Antioch was
slain in the war, except for Mary, for she did
not fight, as she was in pregnancy and birth
with Emma when the war began
.15

Most of the second generation were very small
children when the war began, the oldest being
Arnold and the youngest being Emma, being born
during the war
.16


 The war was long and bloody, with many
brutal casualties
.17

The lake turned maroon as blood ran through it,
and though the forces were small, they fought
for a long while
.18

It was strange for how long they fought, 10
days and 10 nights, with only 8 on each side
.19

But they did, and only Mary was left
.20


 It was very hard on Mary to raise 13
children, but she did so stunningly and gracefully
.21

She was a wonderful mother to them all
.22

Often she would teach them of wondrous things
that would help them in life, for she was very smart
.23


 And sadly, after 19 years of mothering the
children, Mary passed away from fatigue and lack
of good medicine, and the children were on their own
.24


 And it was left to the second generation of
Antioch to replenish the earth, and so they did
and married
.25

The marriages went as follows: Tiffany married
Hellen, Christina married Sebastian, Tyler
married Jamie, Emma married Ronan, Lauren married
Sarah and Liam (at once), and Sophia was married
to Arnold
.26


 And their children went as follows: Christina
and Sebastian had Anne, Ryan, and Natalie
.27

Tyler and Jamie had Carmen, Jordan, Stephanie,
and Lily
.28

Emma and Ronan had Ivan, Jackson, and Julius
.29

Lauren, Sarah, and Liam had Natasha and Sawyer
.30

Sophia and Arnold had 7 entire children, and
gave two of these children off to Tiffany and
Hellen, who got Allen and Dylan,while Sophia
and Arnold kept Isaiah, Ian, Wilson, Phoebe,
and Eden
.31

And thus was the third generation of the
children of Antioch
.32
