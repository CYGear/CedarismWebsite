Ritus Debiti (Proper Rituals) Chapter 1

 In the times after The First True Holy Church
was built, the Cedarists prospered
.1

Their farms were plentiful, and the river, the
“Aqua Cedrina”, flowed greatly through the land
.2

The church was attended by all on the friday
worships, and everyone watched the Prophetai
speak, and ate, and enjoyed themselves, for it
was a joyous time
.3

 And it was at this time that The Lord Cedar
felt he might enlighten the Prophetai Dorinda
with the knowledge of its own proper rituals,
and how to properly do things within Cedarism
.4

And so it was in the night that Cedrus visited
Dorinda, in its human form, and it appeared to
Dorinda in the form a beautiful woman
.5

And the woman, Cedara, said, “Dorinda, It is I,
Cedrus, Domina Cedrus, The Lord, the Lady Cedar
.6

I have knowledge, which you must give to the
others, of the proper rituals and things to do
to your kin when their time to pass or be born
has come
.7

Listen attentively and take note.”
.8

And Dorinda wrote all Domina Cedrus said
meticulously down onto her paper, and
transferred it to Soren, who copied it and put
it into the archives
.9
