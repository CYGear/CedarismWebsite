Chapter 2

 And The Lady Cedar said to Dorinda: “Of the
rituals at birth to perform, I say this:
.1

 Should you want your child to love the church
dearly, with their heart, then you shall find a
nonsharp branch that has fallen onto the ground,
and you shall brush your child's chest in the
shape of a hexagon with it
.2

 If you or a loved one accidentally harms a tree,
it is not too late to revoke Mariana’s claim on
your soul
.3

Simply prepare a bath, soak the branches you
have cut in it, and then let them dry
.4

DO NOT use these branches for your own purposes,
instead bury them underground and plant a tree
seed at their resting place
.5

 To cleanse your child and make them a true
Cedarist, simply take the oils of a tree, and dab
a small amount on their forehead line
.6

 To ensure your loved one goes to The Holy Garden
after death, lay them against your church’s tree,
and surround them with the fallen petals of the
nearby plants
.7

Kneel before the tree and pray to your lord for
their safe passing into The Garden, for this will
ensure their eternal peace
.8

 Before your meal, meditate and think of The Lord
Cedar, all 3 of these meals
.9

At the friday dinner worship, you shall pray this
to The Lord Cedar:
.10

 Dear Lord Cedar, holy creator, I pray to you
that this food is safe to eat and pure, and that
it may benefit my health greatly, and lead me to
a long and healthy life
.11

I give thanks to you for creating this food,
thanks to the farmers that grew it, and thanks to
the kitchen workers that prepared this dish for
my tasting
.12

Thank you for all you have done, and I pray that
you may lead me and my family to happiness in
The Garden
.13

Praise Cedrus!
.14

 When you greet another Cederist, simply bow your
head to them, unless they are your friend or
family
.15

 It will come that on the twenty third day of the
fourth month of the Gregorian calendar you shall
celebrate the creation of the earth, for this was
when the true year began, and when The Lord Cedar
first sparked life in the waters
.16

 And the most important ritual of all, it will be
that come every friday, at the end of your five
days of labor, you shall all gather in the church,
sit and watch The Prophetai and The Bishops recite
The Word to you, and you shall learn and use the
word in your life
.17

And after this you shall have a unified dinner,
all as one, and recite the special prayer Cedrus
has given to you, and this you will do all friday
.18

For if you do not you are not a true Cederist,
for you are a heretic
.19

And all the others shall address you as such,
unless you have been sick to miss the worship
.20

 And this is what The Lady Cedrus said to Dorinda,
and Dorinda wrote it all and gave it to Soren and
Orion quickly, and it was communicated to the
others
.21

And from that point on The Church of Cedarism
followed all of the procedures, with joy, for
their creator had ordained it upon them
.22
