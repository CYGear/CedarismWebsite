Chapter 3

 And with these eternal laws the Church of Cederism
began to create its hierarchy, and divinely worshiped
The Lord Cedar
.1

Friday was appointed as the day of worship,
for after 5 days of labor, the Cederists would thank
their creator and rest for the weekend,
for it was said that such a time was for rest,
not for work
.2

Cedrus did not find working on the fifth, sixth,
or seventh day of the week to be an evil,
but it did not enjoy watching the humans do so,
and so they avoided it
.3

And for their divine worship they found
the word Cederists, following Cedrus,
The Lord Cedar, ruler of the universe,
creator of the Ingens Multiversum,
benevolent god
.4

And so they lived divinely and constructed
their hierarchy, having the lorekeeper scribe
their information hereby translated and made
succinctly available to the general masses
.5
