Divinae Leges (The Divine Laws) Chapter 1

 And in the fifth generation of the Tribe of
Antioch, there was a special child born among
the many
.1

The child's name was Orion, and Orion grew to
be a prophet, or Sancte Propheta, for he had a
special connection with Cedrus no others had,
and so The Lord Cedar often spoke its word to
Orion, and Orion would share The Lord Cedar’s
word at the temple, on the meetings on the fifth
day of the week to celebrate the end of hard work
.2


 And all the others believed in Orion’s power,
and he gave them The Lord Cedar’s holy word, for
they worshipped their creator Cedrus greatly,
for without it they would be dead
.3
