Chapter 2

 And one day it came that The Lord Cedar had
thought of a good governing set of rules for its
creations, and so it decided to tell Orion of
its divine laws
.1

And Orion set these to paper, and presented them
to the tribe as the holy word, for they were the
holy word
.2

For they had been divinely given by The Lord
Cedar itself, and thus began the law
.3

And The Lord Cedar said to Orion: “Of the
treatment of your peers, I say this: You shall
not kill your peers, you shall not injure your
peers, you shall not betray or cheat your peers,
and you shall not commit an evil against your peers
.4

You shall not hurt, but you may restrain your
peers within reason
.5


 You shall not ever harm a tree, bush, or
shrub under penalty of death, this applies for
all plants, for you shall worship them rather
than injuring them
.6

If the plant has passed of natural causes, you
may use its resources for your purposes, but
never for anything that forsakes the will of
The Lord Cedar
.7

You may eat the fruit of a tree or plant, for
its fruit was made for consumption, but never
harm it in the process
.8


 You will always follow the will of The Lord
Cedar, if you do not, you shall be struck down
and punished
.9


 All people are equal, no trait of a person
can make them unequal to another
.10

You shall never discriminate against a person,
except in choosing the best person physically
capable of a job
.11

You shall never limit a person’s love, for their
love and gender is not your concern, and you
shall respect them, unless their love violates
the law of the land
.12


 Never strike down another’s beliefs, unless
it has been proven in the physical world wrong
.13


 Always remember that similarities, in others,
are greater than their differences
.14

If they follow a different religion, even if you
know it to be wrong, set it aside, and always
follow the law of the land, except when it
directly contradicts the word of The Lord Cedar
.15

Shortly after these first rules, more began to
follow Cedarism
.16

Soon, the time had come for the first cedarist
institution to rise from the chaos
.17

Under the guidance of Cedrus’s word, the
following tenants for a church began to emerge
.18


 The Cedarist church exists only to spread
the word of The Lord Cedar
.19

It is never to interfere with our way of life,
unless The Lord Cedar has deemed it necessary
.20


 At the forefront of cedarism lies the
ecclesiastical lorekeeper
.21

They shall maintain and curate the word of The
Lord Cedar
.22


 Behind shall follow the Prophetae Sancti
.23

Their role is to speak the holy word and convey
its role
.24


 Below the prophets are the archbishops, who
manage all bishops, who shall operate all
Cedarist churches, which shall harbour the followers
.25


 The last tenant of the Cederist church
structure is there shall be no militant arm of
the church
.26

Any member can spread the word on a mission,
but dedicated missionaries are forbidden
.27

With the tenants established, the Sancte
Propheta Orion elected his most faithful
follower, one named Soren, to lead as the Lorekeeper
.28

And Soren wrote the word of The Lord Cedar as
transcribed to him by the Prophetae Sancti, and
wrote many of the books hereby scripted to you
.29

And the word of The Lord Cedar survived through
him, passing onto the future, to tell them of
their creator
.30
