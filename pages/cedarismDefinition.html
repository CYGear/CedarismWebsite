Cederism is the belief that The Lord Cedar,
or Cedrus, created the universe and occupies
most of its space, holding the earth/milky
way in his palm. The Holy Book of Cedar comes from
translations and organizations of different
teachings from the church over the years,
as well as the chronological story of
The Church of The Lord Cedar and Cedarism
as a religion. Currently not all books are
in a readable english state, however more
come to the website regularly. We hope you
enjoy the word of The Lord Cedar,
and as always, Praise Cedrus!
