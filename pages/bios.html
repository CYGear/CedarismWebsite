Orion

Orion was the first prophet of the Church of Cederism.
An extremely important figure,
he helped build the church and its morals
through The Lord Cedar’s word, writing the
worship procedures and evils and goods of the church.
He lived until the 13th generation,
when sickness overtook him.

Soren

Soren was the first Ecclesiastical Lorekeeper,
writing The Lord Cedar’s word as the prophets told him.
He is responsible for the writing of most
of the church documents and procedures and scripted
and drew the surviving blueprints of
The First Church of Holy Cedar as found at the end
of Malum Surgit.

Gianni

Gianni was the most supreme archbishop,
and was responsible for the actual construction
and design of The First Church of Holy Cedar.
He made the blueprints and had Soren draft them.

Dorinda

Dorinda was the second prophet after Orion,
and helped interpret the prophetic vision of
the tomb of Mariana’s treachery.

Samuel

Samuel was a bishop who accompanied the troup
on the journey to the ruins in the north.
He helped translate the ancient
message written on the walls.

Mariana

Mariana is the Queen of Evil,
and Ruler of The Dead. She had been forced
into these positions by The Lord Cedar
after she committed unsacred acts
of Heresy and Evil. She was forced into
eternal life and torture due to these actions.

Tyler

Tyler was a member of the second generation
of the Tribe of Antioch. He was the first
true evil commiter, as he killed Sebastian
over a dispute in the fields.
He was the cause of the creation of
The Forest of The Dead and the
eternal tree punishment.

Sebastian

Sebastian was a member of the second
generation of the Tribe of Antioch.
He was murdered by Tyler in the fields.

Mary

Mary was the only first generation
survivor of the War on The River.
She raised the second generation of
the Tribe of Antioch and passed
away 19 years after the war.
